# Trust and authority plan

## Current truthful foundation

- The site is identified as Kapil Shah and focuses on practical cybersecurity for small businesses without a security team.
- An About page describes the purpose and source-first editorial approach without claiming credentials, certifications, awards, client work, or professional status.
- Existing guides show author, publication date, and update date where supplied, and cite primary sources such as NIST and CISA.
- Article markup identifies a Person author and does not claim a company, reviews, ratings, or product testing.

## Editorial process to adopt

For each guide, retain a named author, original publication date, and materially updated date. Keep claims tied to primary references; record the source and the date reviewed when guidance is time-sensitive. Review security-sensitive content at least when cited vendor or government guidance changes, and correct inaccuracies transparently. Separate verified product facts from editorial assessment and information that could not be verified.

## Site information to add when available

- A real contact method and who handles corrections.
- Privacy policy that matches actual analytics, comments, cookies, and data handling before enabling those services.
- Affiliate disclosure before any affiliate relationship or link is added. Clearly label commercial links and explain that editorial recommendations remain the site's own.
- For any future product review: hands-on testing method and dates, supported platforms, verified current pricing with source/date, pros and limitations, suitable customer, alternatives, and clear separation of fact from opinion.

Do not publish unsupported certifications, credentials, endorsements, ratings, review counts, vendor relationships, prices, awards, or availability. Do not add Review/Product structured data unless the visible page genuinely satisfies the relevant requirements.
