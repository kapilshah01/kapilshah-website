## Editorial notes

- Intent labels use the brief's categories: INFORMATIONAL, COMMERCIAL INVESTIGATION, TRANSACTIONAL, and NAVIGATIONAL. The ideas above are informational or commercial investigation; do not force transactional pages without a real product offering and truthful disclosure.
- Audience means the primary reader; funnel stage is awareness, consideration, decision, action, or retention as applicable.
- Add source links, named author, published/updated dates, and review notes, especially for advice affected by changing vendor interfaces or security guidance.
- Topic overlap candidates to resolve during drafting: the phishing guide and payment verification / business email compromise; MFA rollout and MFA recovery; backup plan and recovery preparation; quarterly review and risk register. Use distinct user problems or consolidate where needed.
