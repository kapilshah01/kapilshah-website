## Route and content findings

- The checklist article is imported into `src/lib/content.ts`, has `draft: false`, appears in `getAllArticles()`, and is returned by `generateStaticParams()`. `dynamicParams = true` additionally permits runtime content slugs; missing slugs call `notFound()`. Source inspection does not explain a production 404. Likely causes to investigate against deployment logs include stale deployment/build output, a URL typo, or a deployment artifact that omitted the MDX route. Confirm the origin response after deployment.
- There is no `trailingSlash` override; canonical route forms are slashless and Next.js owns normalization. No broad redirects were added because no legacy URLs were evidenced.
- The six groupings on `/guides` are sections, not category archive routes. Several currently have no articles; this audit keeps them non-URL sections and does not create thin archives.
- Navigation previously used homepage fragment URLs from every route. Footer labels also all linked to `#top`, including nonexistent Tools, Resources, Contact, Privacy, and Terms pages. Header/footer now link only to real routes.
- Articles have explicit relevant related slugs and a relevance-scored fallback. Every published guide links to the guide index through breadcrumbs and to other guides through related links.

## Metadata and structured data

- Global metadata sets the production metadata base, site identity, homepage canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, and index/follow defaults.
- Important routes have distinct titles/descriptions/canonicals. Article pages now have canonical absolute URLs constrained to the production origin, article Open Graph/Twitter data, and index/follow metadata.
- Existing WebSite, Article, and BreadcrumbList markup was retained. Articles now use BlogPosting and identify the visible author as a Person. Global Person markup reflects the existing named author/site identity; it makes no credentials or organization claims.
- No article cover images exist, so schema does not emit an image and Twitter falls back to a summary card. There is no fabricated image, rating, review, or Product markup.

## Internal link audit

- Incoming routes for the checklist include homepage featured card, `/guides`, `/small-business`, `/start-here`, and related-guide links.
- MFA, password policy, and phishing guides appear in `/guides`; the related-guide graph connects all four guides.
- `/small-business` and `/start-here` both link to real guide URLs. Generic links to `/guides` remain where no detailed guide exists yet.
- Old shared header/footer fragment links were unreliable from nested routes; corrected to actual paths.
- No automated graph checker is configured. Recheck incoming links whenever content is added; the registry's `relatedSlugs` is the explicit editorial relationship source.

## Media, performance, accessibility

- No `public/` directory or public image assets are present. Articles have no cover images. There are no image dimensions, responsive image, or alt-text defects to report.
- Server Components are the default. The comments section is the only client component identified, and it is justified by loading/submitting comments. No analytics or third-party scripts were found in inspected source. No risky performance rewrite was warranted.
- Primary and mobile navigation have accessible names; mobile uses native `details/summary`. Main article headings use H1 and MDX content headings provide section structure. Comment controls require a separate form-level verification during browser QA.

## Missing pages, metadata, and follow-up

- About, Contact, Privacy, and Terms were represented by misleading footer links but had no routes. A truthful About page is now present; do not publish contact or legal routes until their content is supplied.
- No title/description or canonical gaps were found for the listed public routes. The home page has metadata through the root layout.
- No product review functionality, affiliate links, product data, or product schema exists. Future formats and disclosure needs are documented in `TRUST_AND_AUTHORITY_PLAN.md`.
- Live status codes, redirects, production HTML canonical output, Search Console coverage, and deployment parity cannot be established from source alone. Check them after deploying the built change.

## Recommendations

1. Deploy through the existing approved process, then request the checklist URL and sitemap from production and confirm 200 responses.
2. Register `https://kapilshah.com.np/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and inspect canonical/indexing reports for the checklist, guide index, and new About route.
3. Add source-backed supporting guides before creating additional pillar landing routes.
4. Add a genuine contact channel and actual privacy/disclosure pages before collecting personal data or monetizing recommendations.
